# Logo Generation Prompt for WHAASCO

Use this prompt with ChatGPT (or other AI image generation tools) to create a modern logo for WHAASCO:

---

## Logo Design Prompt

**Create a modern, professional logo for WHAASCO (West Hartford African American Social & Cultural Organization), a non-profit organization established in 1979 in West Hartford, Connecticut.**

### Design Requirements:

1. **Organization Name**: WHAASCO
   - Full name: West Hartford African American Social & Cultural Organization
   - Acronym should be prominently featured

2. **Color Palette** (Pan-African Colors):
   - **Green** (#3a9b5f): Representing the land and natural wealth
   - **Red** (#ef4444): Representing the blood of ancestors
   - **Black** (#1a1a1a): Representing the people
   - **Gold** (#f59e0b): Representing wealth and prosperity
   - Use these colors thoughtfully and meaningfully

3. **Design Style**:
   - Modern, clean, and professional
   - Suitable for both digital and print use
   - Should work well at various sizes (from favicon to large banners)
   - Contemporary but respectful of cultural heritage

4. **Visual Elements to Consider**:
   - Incorporate Pan-African symbolism (could include stripes, patterns, or geometric elements)
   - Consider community/family unity themes
   - Educational or cultural elements (books, heritage symbols, community connections)
   - Should feel welcoming and inclusive
   - Avoid overly complex designs that won't scale well

5. **Typography**:
   - Bold, readable font
   - "WHAASCO" should be clear and prominent
   - Full organization name can be included in smaller text or as a tagline

6. **Logo Variations Needed**:
   - Primary logo (full color)
   - Horizontal version (for headers/navigation)
   - Icon/mark only (for favicons, social media)
   - Single color version (for printing on dark backgrounds)

7. **Context & Organization Details**:
   - **Founded**: 1979 by a small group of African American residents in West Hartford, CT
   - **First President**: Mrs. Ida McKinney
   - **Mission**: Promote unity, cultural awareness, and community involvement by providing programs that strengthen families, support children, and foster pride in African American heritage and history
   - **Key Achievements**:
     - 2003-2004: Led effort to replace headstone of Bristow (enslaved person who purchased freedom in 1776), which led to naming of Bristow Middle School
     - Advocates for hiring qualified African American personnel in town employment
     - Presents educational programs on African American history and culture
     - Organizes cultural events, family gatherings, and community outreach
   - **Focus Areas**: Family support, youth development, cultural celebration, education, community advocacy
   - **Values**: Unity, cultural pride, family empowerment, educational excellence, community engagement
   - **Tone**: Professional, welcoming, culturally proud, community-focused, educational

8. **Technical Specifications**:
   - Vector format preferred (SVG, AI, or EPS)
   - High resolution for print (300 DPI minimum)
   - Transparent background option
   - Square format for social media compatibility

### Design Inspiration Keywords:
- Pan-African heritage
- Community unity
- Family support
- Cultural pride
- Educational empowerment
- Modern nonprofit branding
- West Hartford community

### What to Avoid:
- Overly complex designs
- Clichéd or stereotypical imagery
- Designs that won't work in black and white
- Fonts that are hard to read at small sizes
